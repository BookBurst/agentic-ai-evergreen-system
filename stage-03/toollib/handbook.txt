MAPLE STREET TOOL LIBRARY: MEMBER HANDBOOK

Membership

Anyone who lives within the city limits can join the library by showing a photo ID and a piece of mail with their address. The annual fee is $30 for one adult. A household of up to four people pays $45. Members who volunteer at least eight hours in a year pay no fee the following year, and the volunteer hours are logged at the front desk.

Borrowing limits

Each member can borrow up to three tools at a time. Hand tools and garden tools are lent for seven days. Power tools are lent for three days, because they are in high demand. Large items such as the tile saw, the wet vacuum and the 20-foot ladder are lent for one day only and must be reserved at least two days ahead.

Renewals are allowed once, for the same number of days as the original loan, as long as nobody else has reserved the tool. Renewals can be made by phone or at the front desk. Items on the large-item list cannot be renewed under any circumstances, and a member who keeps one past the due date loses large-item privileges for ninety days.

Late fees

A late fee of $2 per day applies to every tool kept past its due date, up to a maximum of $20 per tool. After the maximum is reached, the member cannot borrow anything else until the tool is returned and the fee is paid. The library does not charge late fees on days when it is closed. A member who calls before the due date to say a tool will be late can ask for a one-day grace period, which is granted once per member per year.

Damaged and lost tools

Normal wear is expected and costs the member nothing. If a tool is returned damaged through misuse, the member pays the cost of the repair or, if the repair costs more than the tool is worth, the replacement cost. Repair costs are set by the shop volunteer, whose decision can be appealed to the board within thirty days.

A tool that is lost is billed at its replacement cost. The member can instead replace it with the same model, new, within fourteen days of reporting it lost. Members who report a loss within one day of noticing it are never charged the late fee on that tool.

Safety requirements

Before borrowing any power tool for the first time, a member must complete the fifteen-minute safety briefing at the front desk. The briefing is valid for two years. The table saw, the router and the angle grinder also require a signed safety card, which is kept on file. Members under eighteen cannot borrow power tools, even with a parent's signature, but they can use hand tools and garden tools under the same rules as adults.

Protective equipment such as goggles and gloves is lent free with every power tool and must be returned with it. The library does not lend tools for use on roofs, in trenches or near live electrical wiring. A member who states that a tool will be used for one of these purposes will be refused the loan.

Reservations

Reservations can be made online or at the front desk up to fourteen days ahead. A reserved tool is held for the member for twenty-four hours after the pickup time. After that the hold is released and the tool goes back on the shelf. A member who misses three reservations in a row cannot make new reservations for thirty days.

Hours

The library is open Tuesday through Saturday, from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., except on public holidays. Returns can be made through the drop slot after hours, but only hand tools and garden tools can be returned that way. Power tools and large items must be handed over at the desk so the volunteer can check them.
