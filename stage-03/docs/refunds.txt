Refund policy

Clients can request a full refund within 14 days of the first payment, as long as no design files have been delivered yet.

After design files are delivered, refunds are limited to 50 percent of the amount paid for the unfinished part of the project.

Refund requests must be sent by email to billing@brightwater.example. Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 10 business days.
